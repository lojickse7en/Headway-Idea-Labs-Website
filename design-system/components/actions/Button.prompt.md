Headway's primary call-to-action — use for every "Schedule Your Free Consultation" style action.

```jsx
<Button href="#contact">Schedule Your Free Consultation</Button>
<Button variant="outline">Inquire About Partnership</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (orange gradient — the default, one per section), `outline` (white hairline, only on the dark navy band), `outlineNavy` (light backgrounds), `slate` (footer newsletter Sign Up).

Critical rule: the gradient does **not** darken or change on hover. Hover = glow (`--hw-glow-button`) + 2px lift only.
