The model layers into your current programs rather than replacing what already works—guiding local founders, outside founders, and alumni through one clear path from pre-accelerator to post-program.

Who it’s built for

Designed for accelerators, not one-off consulting.

Headway works through innovation hubs, incubators, university programs, economic development agencies, and mission-aligned startup ecosystems.

Structured founder development

Repeatable curriculum creates consistency across cohorts and makes it easier for mentors to stay aligned.

Lower program burden

Standardized tools reduce the amount of founder management that falls back on program teams.

Why this exists

The missing layer after demo day.

Accelerators often tell founders what to do. Headway helps founders learn how to do the work with the strategic clarity, maturity, and proficiency that investors expect.

Programming continuity

Support does not end when a cohort ends. The series keeps founders, mentors, and ecosystem partners moving through one connected readiness path.

Founder transformation

The focus is not just the idea or pitch deck. The goal is to develop founders into purpose-driven business strategists who can lead fundable companies.

Capital discipline

Founders build milestone-based capital plans, financial clarity, investor narratives, and accountability systems before they enter serious investor conversations.
